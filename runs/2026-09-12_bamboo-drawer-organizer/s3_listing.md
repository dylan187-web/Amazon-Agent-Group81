## 长描述

Opening the silverware drawer should not mean digging for a knife that slid under a divider. This expandable drawer organizer is designed around the problems buyers mention most about bamboo trays: joints that come apart, slots too short for dinner knives, and widths that drift after you set them.

In an everyday kitchen, it works as a silverware drawer organizer for forks, spoons and dinner knives, with deeper compartments that keep a full set in place. In a wider drawer, extend it to cover the space and lock the width, and it becomes a kitchen utensil drawer organizer for ladles, spatulas and tongs. Outside the kitchen, the same tray sorts pens and chargers in a desk drawer or brushes and hair ties in a bathroom drawer.

Like other flatware organizers in this style, it is made to be measured first: check your drawer against the size below before ordering.
